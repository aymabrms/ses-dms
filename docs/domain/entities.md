# Candidate Domain Entities

This document evaluates domain entities from the source questionnaires and executive summary. It is not a table design and does not define Prisma models.

| Entity | Business meaning | Source evidence | Why it may deserve its own entity | Important conceptual attributes | Important relationships | Classification | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Project | Infrastructure project or project segment being surveyed | All questionnaires reference TLR/MRP: TDD Segment | Groups survey areas, questionnaires, assignments, and reports | Name, segment, description, status | Has many survey areas, questionnaire versions, assignments | Core | Likely |
| SurveyArea | Geographic survey unit such as barangay | Interview record has Barangay; executive summary groups source/status | Enables assignment, monitoring, filtering, and progress reporting | Barangay, locality, project reference | Belongs to project; has interviews | Core | Likely |
| SurveyAssignment | Work assigned to enumerator/reviewer | Not explicit in paper forms; required by system vision | Needed for mobile offline work and monitoring | Assignee, area, module, due date, status | Project/survey area assigned to user/enumerator | Transactional | Tentative |

## Candidates not accepted as standalone entities yet

- `AssociatedStructure` may be a relationship between two `Structure` records rather than a standalone entity.
- `Tenant` may be a `PersonRole` plus a tenancy relationship, not a separate person-like entity.
- `Occupation`, `Skill`, and financial institutions are likely reference concepts, but raw captured values must be preserved until classification is reviewed.
- `Enumerator` may be a role of `User`; the paper source captures only an enumerator name, so a direct `User` link should not erase the captured text.
