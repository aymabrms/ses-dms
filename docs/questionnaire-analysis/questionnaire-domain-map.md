## Modeling notes

- Questionnaire sections are not primary domain boundaries. Interview, person, household, business, land, structure, tenancy, livelihood, project awareness, and review are reusable domains.
- The source documents use fixed paper rows for household members, business employees, associated structures, tenants, issues, recommendations, benefits, and preferences. The system should support variable-length records or collections.
- The executive summary reports household heads and household members separately. The source questionnaire can capture household head details both in the head section and in the household member table, so reporting rules must define whether and how those records are merged or counted.
